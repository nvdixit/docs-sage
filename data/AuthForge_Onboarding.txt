Welcome! We are thrilled to see your team building innovative products for our 
company. As the central guardians of corporate identity and security, our

perimeter impenetrable. 

This guide outlines the exact, mandatory path your development team must follow 
to integrate your application with **AuthForge**, our new enterprise-wide 
Single Sign-On (SSO) and Identity Management platform. Please read this manual 
carefully before writing any integration code.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
SECTION 1: THE ONBOARDING LIFECYCLE AT A GLANCE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

To ensure a smooth launch, every application must move through four structured 
gates managed by our team:

  [ Gate 1: Registration ] ---> [ Gate 2: Protocol Selection & Dev ]
                                          |
  [ Gate 4: Production Go-Live ] <--- [ Gate 3: Security & Staging Audit ]

Do not attempt to bypass Gate 3. Applications lacking central security clearance 
will have their client credentials automatically revoked by our perimeter gatekeepers.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
SECTION 2: GATE 1 — APPLICATION REGISTRATION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Before touching any code, you must formally register your application in the 
AuthForge Admin Console. This establishes your application's identity within 
our ecosystem.

1. Access the Console: 
   Navigate to https://authforge.internal.company.com using your corporate SSO.
2. Submit Application Metadata:
   - Application Name: (e.g., "Customer Billing Portal")
   - Owning Engineering Squad: (e.g., "Squad-Billing-Core")
   - Primary Slack Contact Channel: (e.g., "#billing-alerts")
   - Environment-specific Redirect URIs: Define strict callback endpoints. 
     Wildcards are strictly prohibited in production URIs.

Upon successful registration, our automated provisioning pipeline will assign 
your application a unique Client ID and generate a cryptographically secure 
Client Secret. Store this secret immediately in your team's vault (HashiCorp 
Vault or AWS Secrets Manager). Never hardcode secrets in your source repository.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
SECTION 3: GATE 2 — PROTOCOL SELECTION & IMPLEMENTATION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

AuthForge supports two industry-standard protocols. Choose the one that fits 
your architecture:

OPTION A: OpenID Connect (OIDC) [RECOMMENDED]
Best for modern web applications, Single Page Applications (SPAs), and mobile apps.
- Issuer URL: https://authforge.internal.company.com/oauth/v1
- Grant Types Supported: Authorization Code with PKCE (mandatory for SPAs/Mobile).

OPTION B: SAML 2.0
Best for legacy enterprise applications and pre-packaged third-party software.
- IdP Entity ID: https://authforge.internal.company.com/saml/v1
- Metadata XML: Downloadable directly from your AuthForge application dashboard.

*Implementation Support:*
We maintain official SDK wrappers and sample boilerplates for Node.js, Python, 
Java, and Go. Visit our internal GitHub organization (`github.internal/iam-samples`) 
before writing custom protocol parsers.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
SECTION 4: ROLE-BASED ACCESS CONTROL (RBAC) & CLAIMS MAPPING
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Your application should not manage its own user directory. Instead, trust 
AuthForge to deliver user attributes and group memberships via ID tokens (OIDC) 
or assertions (SAML).

1. During registration, declare what groups or custom claims your app requires 
   (e.g., `groups: ["finance-editors", "global-admins"]`).
2. Map these claims inside your application middleware to protect routes or 
   grant administrative privileges.
3. If your application requires custom user attributes (e.g., Cost Center ID, 
   Region Code), submit a ticket to our team to review data privacy compliance 
   before requesting them in your scopes.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
SECTION 5: GATE 3 — STAGING INTEGRATION & SECURITY AUDIT
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Once your code is integrated, you must validate it in our Staging environment 
(`authforge-staging.internal.company.com`) before requesting production access.

Our security automated scanner and manual reviewers will inspect your app for:
- Proper token validation and signature verification.
- Secure handling of refresh tokens and logout endpoints (Single Logout / SLO).
- Absence of exposed secrets in client-side bundles (especially for SPAs).

Once your staging test suite passes and our automated compliance scan returns 
"GREEN", you will be cleared for production deployment.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
SECTION 6: GATE 4 — PRODUCTION GO-LIVE & SUPPORT
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

With Gate 3 cleared, our team will promote your application profile to the 
Production AuthForge cluster. 

Operational Support Model:

- Critical Production Outages (24/7): Page the Central IAM On-Call rotation via PagerDuty (`IAM-Core-Service`).

We are excited to partner with your team to make authentication secure, scalable,
